Executive Summary

In banking and structured corporate environments, voucher processing must be timely, traceable, and tightly governed. Manual approval chains, fragmented email trails, incomplete financial records, and labor-intensive callover routines often create delays, reduce visibility, and increase compliance risk. This proposal recommends the implementation of a centralized E-Voucher Workflow Approval System for CMBank to digitize voucher processing from initiation to final settlement while also supporting automatic callover after upload of the bank's XLS callover file.

The proposed solution will support controlled approval routing across key functions such as Initiator, Operations, Finance Control, Internal Audit, Executive Approval, Posting, and Settlement. It also introduces automated callover handling to improve reconciliation speed and reduce manual review workload. Just as importantly, it creates a searchable digital record so vouchers, callover files, and supporting documents can be tracked and accessed quickly even after several years, instead of spending hours, days, or longer searching through physical files and paper archives. The result is a more efficient and transparent process that strengthens internal control and provides management with better oversight of operational and finance-related workflows.

Business Context

Current Challenges

  • Manual or semi-manual voucher approvals that create delays across departments.
  • Limited visibility into voucher status, approver actions, and pending bottlenecks.
  • Difficulty maintaining complete records for audit, compliance, and financial review.
  • Weak coordination between Operations, Finance Control, Internal Audit, and executive approvers.
  • The current callover process is rigorous, bulky, difficult, and expensive because it depends heavily on printed paper, physical vouchers, and slow manual review.
  • Searching for old documents, callover records, and vouchers can take hours, days, or even longer when records are stored manually.

Strategic Need

  • Digitize internal voucher processing without weakening governance controls.
  • Standardize approvals across units, departments, or branches.
  • Improve accountability with role-based ownership at every stage.
  • Support internal audit readiness and stronger financial documentation.
  • Automate callover handling from structured XLS uploads to improve speed and accuracy.
  • Enable quick and reliable access to vouchers, callover records, and supporting documents across several years.

Proposed Solution

TSoft Computers Inc. proposes a secure web-based E-Voucher Workflow Approval System that enables authorized staff to create vouchers, attach supporting documents, route requests through approval stages, capture accounting details, and track status through to posting and settlement.

In addition, the system includes an automatic callover feature that performs callover processing after the upload of the bank's XLS callover file. This makes the platform especially suited to CMBank because it combines workflow efficiency with the control discipline expected in a regulated financial institution.

The solution will be deployed on secure hosting infrastructure and will use a secure PostgreSQL database for structured financial records together with a secure Backblaze B2 S3-compatible bucket for voucher attachments, supporting documents, and uploaded callover files.

Callover Flow

The system supports a structured callover process that starts with the CBA-provided Excel file and ends with matched and reviewable records inside the E-Voucher platform.

1. CBA XLS File The callover spreadsheet is received from CBA in the approved Excel format.
2. Upload to E-Voucher An authorized user uploads the XLS file into the E-Voucher system.
3. System Reads File The platform opens the spreadsheet and reads the transaction rows and configured fields.
4. Data Extraction References, dates, narration, amounts, and other matching values are extracted.
5. Voucher Matching The extracted entries are compared against voucher records already stored in the system.
6. Exception Flagging The system marks items as matched, unmatched, or exception cases for follow-up.
7. Review & Confirmation Operations, Finance, or Audit users review the callover results and resolve outstanding items.
8. History Updated The final callover outcome is stored in the voucher record and audit history.

What the system reads

  • XLS rows received from CBA for callover review.
  • Transaction references, amounts, dates, narration, and related identifiers.
  • Mapped voucher numbers or other configured matching keys.

What the system produces

  • Automatic comparison between uploaded callover data and voucher records.
  • Visibility into matched items, pending items, and exceptions requiring review.
  • A permanent audit log showing when the file was uploaded, read, checked, and concluded.
